Can I order multiple different products in one cart?+

Yes. Add any combination of ELD, asset trackers, vehicle trackers, and dashcams to the same order. Everything runs on one Matrack account — one map, one app, one invoice.

Is there a long-term contract?+

No. All plans are month-to-month. Cancel anytime. 30-day risk-free trial on every order.

How fast does hardware ship?+

All hardware ships within 48 hours of order confirmation. ELD and dashcam units arrive pre-paired to your account, ready to activate on arrival.

What does 'pre-paired' mean?+

Before shipping, we associate each device with your Matrack account. When it arrives, you plug it in — no manual configuration, no activation codes.

Do I need different devices for different vehicle types?+

Generally: OBD for cars/vans/pickups; Hardwire Classic or Silver for semis and heavy equipment; Asset Classic or 3-Yr Sub for unpowered trailers; ELD for HOS compliance.

Is there a volume discount for larger orders?+

Yes. Contact sales for fleet pricing on orders of 5+ devices. Most quotes return same business day.
