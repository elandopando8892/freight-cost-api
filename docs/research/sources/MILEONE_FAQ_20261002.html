Frequently Asked Questions

Got a question? We've got the answers.

Do I need to pay a deposit or sign a lease?

At MileOne, there are no fees, contracts, or deposits required to use our services.

Do you offer day passes for coworking?

We offer day passes for $10. Simply call or stop by to get started. Day pass includes:

- Access to Co-Working from 9am-6pm

- Unlimited Coffee, Water

- High-speed Business WiFi

- Printing Services

How does mentorship work?

At MileOne, we offer in-house marketing and business development guidance for all incubator and launchpad members. We also connect you with a network of experts in different fields to help you grow. M1 Members can set a meeting with us at any time. Not a member yet? We offer an initial strategy session to prospective members at no cost. 

Can my business partner or a guest work with me?

You can meet with guests, clients, and partners using your conference room credits. Anyone looking to work from the space will need to sign up for membership or purchase a day pass. 

Can I host events at MileOne?

Members can host events in our conference rooms that can fit up to 18-20 people.

Do you have free parking?

Parking is always free at MileOne for members, guests of members, and event attendees.

Can I use MileOne as the address for my business?

You can only use the address if you sign up for an Incubator or Launchpad plan. If you qualify, we will assign you a unique suite number to use. 

Can I use the MileOne phone number as my business number?

Unfortunately we do not offer a phone service for businesses.
